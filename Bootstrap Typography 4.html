How Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Everyday Life

Artificial intelligence is becoming a part of our daily lives, from smartphones and education to business and entertainment.

By DailyView Editorial Team · September 1, 2026

Technology has changed the way people work, communicate and learn. In recent years, artificial intelligence has become one of the fastest-growing areas of technology.

From smart assistants to recommendation systems, AI-powered tools are now being used by millions of people every day. Businesses are also using these technologies to automate repetitive tasks and improve productivity.

AI in Education

Major Benefits

  • Personalized learning experiences
  • Improved productivity

The Future of Technology
